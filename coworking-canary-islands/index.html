Why Gran Canaria over other Canary Islands?

All Canary Islands have their charms, but Gran Canaria stands out as the best choice for remote workers and digital nomads. The island offers a perfect balance of modern infrastructure and natural beauty — you get the sand dunes and beaches of the south, the cosmopolitan vibe of Las Palmas, and a well-established coworking scene that keeps growing year after year.

Gran Canaria is also the most connected island in the archipelago: Las Palmas airport (LPA) has daily direct flights from London, Amsterdam, Frankfurt, Madrid, Paris and dozens more cities. Internet infrastructure is excellent across the island, especially in Maspalomas and Las Palmas.

LifeCowork is based in Maspalomas — the resort area in the south of Gran Canaria, famous for its iconic sand dunes and crystal-clear waters. It's the best place on the island for a working holiday: all the amenities you need, without the noise of a big city.
