How to work remotely from Gran Canaria

Setting up your remote work base in Gran Canaria is simpler than you think. Here's the typical path for professionals who come to LifeCowork:

1. Book your spot. Message us on WhatsApp to check availability and pick your plan — Day Pass, Week Pass or Monthly. No paperwork, no waiting lists.

2. Arrive and connect. From the moment you sit down, you have 1Gb fiber internet, a clean ergonomic workstation, coffee and everything else you need. Most people are fully productive within minutes of arriving.

3. Work your normal hours. Gran Canaria runs on GMT+1 (same as Spain, UK in winter, and just one hour off the rest of Central Europe). Your timezone barely changes — you keep your meetings, your deadlines and your rhythm.

4. Enjoy the rest. The beach, the dunes, the restaurants, the year-round sunshine — that's what makes Gran Canaria different from every other coworking setup you've tried.
