Your remote office in the Canary Islands

Remote work feels different when your office is just minutes away from the beach. LifeCowork is a modern coworking space in Maspalomas, Gran Canaria, created for digital nomads, freelancers and remote workers looking for a productive place to work while enjoying the Canary Islands lifestyle.

Located in the south of Gran Canaria, near Playa del Inglés, LifeCowork combines fast 1Gb symmetric fiber internet, 24/7 access, comfortable workstations and an international atmosphere designed for people who work remotely from anywhere in the world.

Forget noisy cafés and unstable hotel WiFi. Here you'll find a professional workspace with meeting rooms, coffee included, optional secondary monitors, chill areas and everything needed for productive workdays under the sun.

Whether you are spending a few days in Gran Canaria or planning a long stay in the Canary Islands, LifeCowork gives you a flexible and reliable base to work, network and stay productive while enjoying one of Europe's best climates all year round.

Why Gran Canaria for Digital Nomads?

Gran Canaria has become one of Europe's top destinations for digital nomads and remote workers thanks to its warm climate, affordable lifestyle and international community.

Especially in the south of the island, areas like Maspalomas and Playa del Inglés attract freelancers and entrepreneurs from all over the world looking for a better work-life balance. The island offers great flight connections, beaches, outdoor activities, coworking culture and a growing remote work ecosystem. For many professionals, Gran Canaria is the perfect place to combine productivity with quality of life.
